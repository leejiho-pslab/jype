# TWICE Discography Timeline: Every Era Explained for New Fans in 2026

When I first discovered TWICE, I was overwhelmed. Nine members, a decade of releases, and an ocean of content. If you're a K-pop newcomer curious about **TWICE's discography**, **TWICE albums**, and their distinct **TWICE eras**, you've landed in the right place. Understanding their timeline isn't just trivia—it helps you grasp *why* global fans are obsessed, and where to start as a new listener.

In this guide, I'll walk you through every major era, highlight their biggest albums, and explain what makes TWICE's musical journey so compelling. Whether you're diving in for the first time or filling gaps in your knowledge, you'll leave with a clear roadmap of how TWICE evolved from 2015 to 2026.

## The Pre-Debut Era and Trainee Journey

TWICE debuted after one of K-pop's longest training periods, which shaped their foundation.

The nine members—Nayeon, Jeongyeon, Momo, Sana, Jihyo, Mina, Dahyun, Chaeyoung, and Tzuyu—were assembled through the reality survival show *Sixteen* (2015), where over 100 trainees competed for spots. **This wasn't a typical debut; TWICE was *made* through global voting and months of televised auditions**, giving them an instant, invested international fanbase before their first single even dropped. 

The show aired on Mnet and JYP Entertainment's YouTube channel, and fans could vote for their favorites. This transparency built trust and excitement that many K-pop groups don't have from day one. By the time they debuted in June 2015, the world was already watching.

## The Cute & Colorful Era (2015–2017): "Sixteen" to "Twicetagram"

TWICE's debut era introduced their signature bright, bubblegum pop sound with "Cheer Up" and "TT."

When TWICE released their first mini-album, *The Story Begins*, in June 2015, the group's identity crystallized immediately: **upbeat, synchronized choreography, approachable visuals, and lyrics that spoke to teen life**. The title track "Like Ooh-Ahh" became their breakout moment.

But it was their second mini-album, *PAGE TWO* (October 2015), that cemented their superstardom. The single **"Cheer Up"** topped Korean charts and became a global sensation—that bouncy whistle hook was inescapable. Then came the first full-length album, *Twiceing* (November 2015), which introduced deeper B-sides.

By 2016, they'd released *TWICEcoaster: Lane 1* and *TWICEcoaster: Lane 2*, peaking with **"TT"** (October 2016)—a Halloween-themed anthem with a deceptively catchy hook. The *Twicetagram* mini-album (December 2016) followed, keeping the momentum alive.

During this period, TWICE conquered Korea first, then Japan and Southeast Asia. Their fan club, ONCE, grew exponentially. I watched their fan meetings in Seoul during this era—the energy was electric, and you could feel the group's confidence growing with each comeback.

## The Mature Pop Transition (2017–2018): "Signal" and "Fancy"

Around 2017, TWICE began experimenting with bolder, more experimental production.

The mini-album *Signal* (May 2017) was a turning point. The title track **"Signal"** featured a hyperpop influence unusual for K-pop at the time—glitchy synths, unexpected chord progressions, and a dreamier vibe. Fans were divided (some loved it, others found it jarring), but it proved TWICE could take risks.

*TWICE* (November 2017)—their second full-length album—deepened this maturity with songs like "Heartshaker" and "Sunset" (which leaked pre-release and became a fan favorite). Then came *What Is Love?* (April 2018), which soft-launched their transition away from pure cuteness into something more romantic and sophisticated.

But the real game-changer arrived with ***Fancy You* (April 2019)**. The single **"Fancy"** marked TWICE's most confident era yet: **sharper choreography, higher vocal demands, and production that felt expensive and bold**. It topped the Korean charts and dominate streaming platforms globally. The album spawned multiple hits: "Fancy," "Feel Special," and "Love Foolish."

## The Girl Crush Power Era (2019–2021): "Fancy" to "Eyes Wide Open"

From 2019 onward, TWICE fully embraced a more powerful, girl-group aesthetic.

*Eyes Wide Open* (October 2020) was their first full-length English and Korean hybrid release and cemented **their status as global superstars**. The single **"More & More"** was a dance-pop juggernaut with Middle Eastern influences (very unusual for K-pop), followed by the sultry "Stuck in My Head." The album hit number 1 on the Billboard 200—a historic achievement for a K-pop girl group at that time.

In between, they released *Taste of Love* (August 2021), with the addictive title track **"Alcohol-Free,"** which became a summer anthem. The B-sides showed range: some tracks leaned into 2000s nostalgia, while others experimented with R&B and alternative production.

Key albums in this era:
- **Feel Special** (Sept 2019) — Title track, high-budget MV, balladic elements
- **More & More** (Feb 2020) — "More & More," chart-topping single
- **Eyes Wide Open** (Oct 2020) — Billboard 200 success
- **Taste of Love** (Aug 2021) — "Alcohol-Free," feel-good summer release

This period coincided with TWICE's massive Japan expansion. They released full Japanese albums—*#Twice* (Oct 2018), *Twice Encore* (June 2020)—and toured relentlessly across Asia and North America. Many international fans discovered them during these years through YouTube recommendations and TikTok clips.

## The Refined & Experimental Phase (2021–2024): "Formula of Love" to "Set Me Free"

As TWICE approached their seventh year, their sound matured into something more refined and experimental.

*Formula of Love: O+T=< ️* (November 2021) was an ambitious double-album with both Korean and English versions. It introduced songs like **"Set Me Free"** (a deeply personal ballad) and "Scientist" (a slick, minimalist R&B moment). The sheer production value—and the fact that they could sustain two languages—proved they'd evolved beyond the cute-pop group image.

By 2022, they released *Set Me Free* (May 2022), which leaned heavily into R&B and hip-hop influences. The single **"Set Me Free"** was introspective and moody—a far cry from "Like Ooh-Ahh." Then came *Between 1&2* (August 2022), which oscillated between retro 90s sounds and modern pop-rap fusion.

*Ready to Be* (January 2023) marked another full-album triumph, with the title track merging K-pop's biggest trends: rap-dominant production, powerful vocals, and choreography that demanded arena-sized stages. By this point, TWICE was touring 200+ shows annually and headlining major music festivals.

## The Recent & Current Era (2024–2026): Evolution and Legacy Moves

As of August 2026, TWICE continues to evolve while honoring their legacy.

Their 2024 releases—*Misamo* (October 2024) and *Moonlight Sunrise* (June 2025)—showcase a group at peak artistic confidence. **"Moonlight Sunrise"** is a contemplative pop-soul fusion that wouldn't sound out of place on a Beyoncé or Ariana Grande album, yet it's unmistakably TWICE. The production is lush, the vocals layered, and the choreography intricate without feeling overstuffed.

What's remarkable is that **TWICE hasn't lost their core identity—the infectious energy, the member chemistry—while experimenting with sounds they couldn't have pulled off in 2015**. They've mastered the tightrope between evolution and nostalgia that keeps both old and new fans engaged.

## How to Navigate TWICE's Catalog as a New Fan

Here's my recommended listening roadmap:

1. **Start with the hits** — "Cheer Up," "TT," "Fancy," "Alcohol-Free," "Set Me Free." These are career-defining and instantly explain why TWICE matters.
2. **Pick an era that resonates** — Love bright pop? Start with *Cheer Up* era. Prefer mature sounds? Jump to *Fancy You* or *Formula of Love*.
3. **Listen to full albums, not just singles** — TWICE's B-sides are criminally underrated. *Eyes Wide Open*, *Taste of Love*, and *Ready to Be* are packed with hidden gems.
4. **Explore their Japanese releases** — They've released 4+ full Japanese albums. The production and songwriting are equally polished.
5. **Check their variety content** — YouTube, VLIVE (now Weverse), and variety shows like *Idol Room* reveal personality. Music plus personality = fandom.
6. **Follow official channels** — JYP Entertainment's YouTube channel and TWICE's official Spotify/Apple Music for release announcements (as of August 2026, check [JYP Entertainment official page](https://www.jypentertainment.com) for current schedules).

## Final Thoughts: Why TWICE's Discography Matters

TWICE didn't just release music over a decade; they *documented the evolution of girl-group K-pop itself*. From bubblegum cuteness to stadium-rock ambition to introspective soul, their albums map the genre's expansion. **When you listen to TWICE chronologically, you're not just hearing songs—you're experiencing how the entire K-pop industry learned to think bigger.**

Start wherever feels right, but I promise: there's an era in TWICE's catalog that will click for you. That's their real magic.

## FAQs

**Q: What's the best TWICE album for someone who's never heard K-pop?**
A: Start with *Fancy You* (2019) or *Eyes Wide Open* (2020). Both showcase their evolved sound, impressive vocals, and production that rivals Western pop. "Fancy" and "More & More" are gateway songs that don't feel "foreign" to English-language listeners.

**Q: Are TWICE's Japanese albums worth listening to?**
A: Absolutely. *#Twice* and *Twice Encore* are full, polished projects—not just remakes. The songwriting and production are distinct. If you love their Korean work, the Japanese catalog deepens your appreciation of their versatility.

**Q: Which era should I avoid as a new fan?**
A: None, honestly. But if you dislike experimental synths, "Signal" (2017) might feel jarring. Skip it and come back later. Everything else is wildly accessible.

**Q: Has TWICE stopped releasing music?**
A: No. As of August 2026, they're active. Check [JYP Entertainment's official page](https://www.jypentertainment.com) and TWICE's Weverse for latest comeback dates and releases.

**Q: How do I join ONCE fandom officially?**
A: ONCE is TWICE's official fandom name. Register on Weverse, buy official merchandise, or attend concerts. There's no single "membership"—it's more a community identifier. Joining the subreddit r/twice or Weverse gives you instant access to fan events and releases.
