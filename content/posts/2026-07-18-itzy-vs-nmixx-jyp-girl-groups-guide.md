If you're new to **JYP girl groups** and feeling overwhelmed by the ITZY vs NMIXX debate, here's the short answer up front: **ITZY is your group if you love confident, high-energy pop with attitude; NMIXX is your group if you want experimental genre-mixing and vocal firepower** — though honestly, there's nothing stopping you from loving both. In this guide I'll walk you through each group's identity, members, discography highlights, fandom culture, and how to actually get into their content as a first-time fan.

---

## Who Are ITZY? JYP's "Self-Love Queens" Explained

ITZY is the group that rewrote the playbook for how a K-pop girl group talks about self-image — their debut single "DALLA DALLA" basically said "I don't care what you think of me," and it landed like a thunderclap in early 2019.

Formed by [JYP Entertainment](https://www.jype.com/), ITZY (pronounced "it-zi," meaning "I have it" in Korean) debuted on **February 12, 2019**, with five members:

| Member | Role | Hometown |
|---|---|---|
| Yeji | Main Dancer, Lead Vocalist | Jeonju, South Korea |
| Lia | Main Vocalist | Seoul, South Korea |
| Ryujin | Center, Lead Dancer | Seoul, South Korea |
| Chaeryeong | Main Dancer, Lead Vocalist | Seoul, South Korea |
| Yuna | Lead Dancer, Vocalist, Maknae | Busan, South Korea |

Their concept has always revolved around **fearlessness, individuality, and not conforming to beauty standards** — which was genuinely refreshing when it arrived. Tracks like "ICY," "Not Shy," "LOCO," and "SNEAKERS" kept the energy consistently high, while their 2022 mini-album *CHECKMATE* showed a darker, more mature side. As of July 2026, ITZY has released multiple studio albums and completed several world tours, cementing them as one of JYP's flagship acts globally.

Their fandom is called **MIDZY** (a blend of "ITZY" and the Korean word for "friend"), and it's a passionate, art-heavy community known for fan projects and concert banners. If you want to get started, the official ITZY content hub lives on [Weverse](https://weverse.io/), where they post member diaries, behind-the-scenes clips, and fan replies.

## Who Are NMIXX? JYP's Most Experimental Group Yet

NMIXX is the group that will make your music-theory-loving friend lose their mind — in the best possible way.

NMIXX (pronounced "en-mix") debuted on **February 22, 2022**, under JYP Entertainment, with a concept the label branded **"MIXX POP"** — a style that fuses multiple genres *within a single track*. Their debut single "O.O" dropped two completely different sonic sections mid-song with no warning, and the internet genuinely didn't know what to do with it. That's the point.

The group currently has six members (as of July 2026):

| Member | Role | Fun Fact |
|---|---|---|
| Lily | Main Vocalist | Canadian-born; trilingual |
| Haewon | Lead Vocalist | Known for powerful live vocals |
| Sullyoon | Visual, Vocalist | Widely praised for stage presence |
| Bae | Main Vocalist | American-born; former trainee in the US |
| Jiwoo | Main Dancer, Vocalist | High-energy performer |
| Kyujin | Main Dancer, Lead Vocalist | Maknae; known for charisma |

*(Note: NMIXX originally debuted as a seven-member group; the lineup has had changes — always check the [official NMIXX page](https://www.jype.com/Artists/NMIXX) for the current roster.)*

Post-debut releases like "DICE," "TANK," and "expergo" pushed the MIXX POP concept further, and by their 2024–2025 era the group had developed a devoted global fanbase called **NSWER** (pronounced "answer"). Their content is also primarily distributed through [Weverse](https://weverse.io/nmixx) and their official YouTube channel.

NMIXX took me longer to appreciate because MIXX POP genuinely requires a few listens. But once it clicks, it *really* clicks. **Think of ITZY as the confident friend who walks into a room like they own it; NMIXX is the friend who shows up and suddenly the room is on fire.**

## How to Actually Get Into ITZY and NMIXX as a New Fan

The K-pop fan onboarding process can feel overwhelming, so here's a step-by-step approach that actually works:

1. **Start with the title tracks.** For ITZY: "DALLA DALLA" → "ICY" → "LOCO" → "CHECKMATE." For NMIXX: "O.O" → "DICE" → "TANK" → "expergo."
2. **Watch the official music videos** on their YouTube channels — [ITZY on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@ITZY) and [NMIXX on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@NMIXX_official). MV comments are a goldmine for understanding the fandom's perspective.
3. **Learn the members through a "member introduction" fan edit** — search "[Group name] member introduction 2026" on YouTube. These are usually five-minute cuts that give you faces, names, and personality highlights.
4. **Explore reality content.** Both groups have variety show appearances archived on YouTube. ITZY's *ITZY? ITZY!* web series and NMIXX's "NMIXX LOG" are great starting points.
5. **Join the fandom on Weverse** — it's free, and members post there directly. Reading fan translations of member posts is genuinely how you develop an attachment to individuals.
6. **Check concert tour announcements** on the [JYP Entertainment official site](https://www.jype.com/) and on each group's Weverse page. Ticket prices and tour schedules change frequently — always verify on official channels before purchasing.

## Fandom Culture: MIDZY vs NSWER — What to Expect

Both fandoms are welcoming to newcomers, but they have distinct personalities worth knowing before you wade in.

**MIDZY** has been around since 2019 and has the polish of a maturing fandom. Expect elaborate fan projects at concerts (lightstick ocean coordination, birthday ad campaigns in Times Square or Piccadilly Circus), a strong presence on X (formerly Twitter) and TikTok, and a culture that emphasizes supporting each member equally. The ITZY official lightstick — a purple and white design — is a staple at live events.

**NSWER** is younger as a fandom but intensely devoted to the "MIXX POP" concept. There's a lot of music-analysis content in this fandom — threads breaking down the chord progressions in "expergo" are genuinely impressive. NSWER tends to skew toward fans who also follow music production, and there's a strong cross-over with fans of art-pop and experimental music internationally.

Both fandoms have had the typical K-pop fandom tensions (shipping discourse, solo stan drama), but the overwhelming majority of both communities are just people who love good music and want to share that enthusiasm. **As a new fan, you'll be welcomed in either corner.**

## Going to a Concert or Fan Meet in Korea: What You Need to Know

Seeing either group live in Seoul is genuinely one of the best K-pop experiences you can have — and for international fans visiting Korea, it's a bucket-list item.

Major ITZY and NMIXX concerts typically take place at venues like **KSPO Dome**, **Jamsil Indoor Stadium**, or **Inspire Arena** in the greater Seoul area. Tickets are sold through platforms like **Melon Ticket**, **YES24 Live**, and **Interpark Ticket** — note that some sales require a Korean phone number or local payment method, so international fans often use fan club pre-sale options or licensed resellers. Always buy from official or well-known platforms to avoid scams.

For travel context, the Korea Tourism Organization's [VisitKorea](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/) website has a dedicated K-pop tourism section with info on fan zones, entertainment district maps (hello, HYBE and JYP in Mapo-gu), and cultural tips for first-time visitors. Prices and tour dates change seasonally — **I always recommend bookmarking both groups' official Weverse pages and JYP's site for the most current announcements.**
