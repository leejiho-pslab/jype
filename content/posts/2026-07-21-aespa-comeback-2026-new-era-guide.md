## How to Stream and Support aespa's Comeback as an International Fan

One of the most common questions I get from readers outside Korea is: *how do I actually help chart performance from overseas?* It matters more than you might think — streaming numbers on Spotify and YouTube, along with digital album sales on platforms like Bugs and Melon (which have international options), all feed into chart tallies that affect the group's visibility.

Here's a practical how-to for international MYs:

1. **Stream the title track on Spotify and Apple Music** — repeat streams within the first 24–72 hours are weighted most heavily for chart debuts.
2. **Watch the MV on YouTube** — aespa's official channel is [SMTOWN's YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@SMTOWN). Watch fully, avoid skipping, and avoid using bots or VPNs (which can get streams invalidated).
3. **Buy the digital album on Bugs** — international purchases are possible with a credit card; Bugs counts toward Korean digital charts.
4. **Vote on fan apps** — Choeaedol, Idol Champ, and similar apps run weekly polls that contribute to show wins (like *Music Bank* or *Inkigayo*). Download them and vote daily during comeback week.
5. **Buy physical albums from official sources** to count toward Hanteo and Circle (formerly Gaon) chart physical sales.
6. **Share and engage on social** — Twitter/X, TikTok, and Instagram engagement boosts algorithmic reach, especially in the first 48 hours post-release.

It sounds like a lot, but even doing two or three of these consistently makes a difference. The Korean music industry tracks these metrics obsessively, and strong international performance genuinely influences where groups tour next.
