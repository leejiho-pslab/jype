## What Is Ulzzang Makeup, and Why Is It Having a 2026 Moment?

Ulzzang (얼짱) literally means "best face" in Korean slang, and the style is all about achieving a youthful, photogenic glow that looks effortless. If you've ever wondered why certain Korean selfies look almost impossibly dewy and luminous — that's ulzzang energy. The term first went viral on early Korean internet forums around 2004–2008, peaked internationally around 2010–2014, and then quietly evolved into mainstream K-beauty. **In 2026, it's back with a vengeance**, but the driver this time isn't forums — it's short-form video. TikTok Korea's #얼짱메이크업 hashtag has racked up hundreds of millions of combined views as of July 2026, and beauty apps like [LOOKS](https://looks.so) are surfacing hyper-personalized ulzzang tutorials to users worldwide. The comeback is real, it's algorithm-driven, and it's global.

## The 5 Ulzzang Makeup Trends Dominating Korean Apps Right Now

Korean beauty creators are converging on a handful of specific techniques this season. Here's a breakdown of what I'm seeing dominate the feeds as of July 2026:

| Trend | Core Technique | Key Product Type |
|---|---|---|
| **Glass Skin Base** | Layered hydration + skin tint | Essence serum + lightweight cushion |
| **Puppy Eye Liner** | Downward-flicked lower lash line | Brown or burgundy eyeliner |
| **Cloud Gradient Lip** | Blurred, ombré center | Lip tint applied with fingertip |
| **Soap Brow** | Laminated, upward-brushed brow | Brow wax or clear brow gel |
| **Lit-From-Within Blush** | High-placed, diffused flush | Liquid blush on cheekbone & nose |

**Glass Skin Base** is the undisputed number-one trend. Korean creators are spending more time on skin prep than on any other step — double cleanse, toner pads, a hydrating essence, then a skin tint or cushion compact layered on top. The goal is a finish that photographs like you're wearing almost nothing. I personally switched to this method in spring 2026 and was shocked at how much more "awake" I looked in photos.

**Puppy Eye Liner** is the direct successor to the cat-eye. Instead of flicking upward and outward, you draw a subtle downward flick on the outer lower lash line to create a soft, doe-eyed effect. It's incredibly flattering and less intimidating for people who struggle with classic liner wings.

**Cloud Gradient Lips** — also called "물들인 입술" (water-soaked lips) in Korean — involve dabbing a lip tint on the center of your lips with your fingertip and blending outward, leaving the edges deliberately softer. It mimics the natural flush you'd get from eating a cherry popsicle, and it photographs beautifully.

## How to Recreate the 2026 Ulzzang Look: A Step-by-Step Guide

The beauty of the 2026 ulzzang aesthetic is that the steps are logical and buildable. Here's the exact sequence Korean creators are following:

1. **Double cleanse** — Oil cleanser first, then a gentle foam cleanser. Skin prep is non-negotiable.
2. **Layer hydration** — Apply a toner (Korean toners are more like essences — watery, not astringent) in 2–3 light pats. Let it absorb.
3. **Serum or ampoule** — A niacinamide or hyaluronic acid serum helps create that glass-skin canvas.
4. **Skin tint or cushion compact** — Apply with fingertips or a damp sponge. Use the absolute minimum amount — you want skin to show through.
5. **Liquid concealer (targeted only)** — Under-eye area and any spots. Skip the full-coverage foundation.
6. **Soap brow** — Swipe a clear brow gel or a wetted brow soap upward through your brows. This instantly modernizes any look.
7. **Puppy eye liner** — Use a fine-tip brown or dark burgundy liner. Draw a tiny downward flick from the outer corner of your lower lash line. Add 2–3 dots beneath for a "lash" illusion.
8. **Gradient lip** — Dab a lip tint on the center of your lips and tap outward with your fingertip until it blurs naturally.
9. **Liquid blush** — Smile, then place a small drop of liquid blush on the apple of your cheek. Blend upward toward the temple — and add a tiny dot on the nose bridge for that flushed, outdoorsy look.
10. **Setting mist** — Finish with a fine setting mist (not a setting spray — the texture is different, much lighter) to lock in dewiness without looking greasy.

The full routine, once you've practiced it a few times, takes about **12–15 minutes**. It's genuinely fast for how polished the result looks.

## The Bigger Cultural Shift Behind the 2026 Ulzzang Revival

**The return of ulzzang isn't just nostalgia — it reflects a genuine shift in how Korean Gen Z thinks about beauty.** After a few years dominated by heavy contouring, bold graphic liner, and maximalist looks (driven partly by the global influence of Western beauty trends), Korean beauty culture in 2025–2026 has swung back toward something softer and more "natural-looking" — even if achieving that natural look takes 15 careful steps. This "no-makeup makeup" tension has always been at the heart of K-beauty, but the 2026 iteration feels more confident about it. The message is: skill should be invisible, and your skin should be the star.

This shift is also visible in how Korean celebrities style themselves for public appearances. Rather than dramatic stage makeup, many K-pop idols and actors are now showing up to fan events and airport arrivals in exactly this kind of understated, glassy, ulzzang-coded look — and their fans screenshot and reverse-engineer every detail within hours.
